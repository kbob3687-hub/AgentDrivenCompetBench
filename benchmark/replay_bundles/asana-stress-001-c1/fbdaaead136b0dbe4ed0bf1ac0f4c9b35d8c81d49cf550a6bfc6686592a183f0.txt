## What's included in Asana

[![Asana Goals](https://images.g2crowd.com/uploads/product/image/2a3c6c39bf8b8eb277cfcd42d9bbca1e/asana.png)\\
\\
Asana GoalsAsana Goals is a dedicated goal-tracking and management capability built natively into the Asana work management platform. It serves as a flexible, single source of truth for setting, tracking, and managing company-wide, team-level, and individual goals — all on the same platform where teams execute their day-to-day work.\\
\\
At its core, Asana Goals bridges the gap between strategic objectives and operational execution. Organizations can define goals as Objectives (broad, high-level outcomes), Key Results (specific, measurable milestones that indicate progress toward an objective), or Individual Goals (goals owned by a single person). Goals can be organized hierarchically: company goals sit at the top, team goals ladder up to them, and sub-goals break large objectives into smaller, actionable pieces.\\
\\
One of the most powerful aspects of Asana Goals is its ability to connect directly to the work that supports each goal. Users can link goals to projects, portfolios, and tasks within Asana, enabling automatic progress updates as work is completed. When a task or milestone is finished, the connected goal's progress updates in real time — eliminating the need for manual status reporting. Up to 100 items (tasks, projects, or sub-goals) can be connected to a single goal for automatic progress tracking.\\
\\
For goals that rely on external data, Asana Goals integrates with Salesforce, allowing users to link a goal to a Salesforce report so that pipeline metrics, deal data, and other CRM insights automatically update goal progress. External data can also be surfaced via Power BI and Looker integrations for deeper analytics and reporting.\\
\\
Progress tracking in Asana Goals supports both automatic and manual update methods. The automatic method calculates progress from connected sub-goals, projects, or tasks. The manual method allows users to set a current value and target value, with measurement options including percentage, number, and currency. A progress-over-time chart on each goal's detail page visualizes historical progress, with clickable data points linking directly to corresponding status updates.\\
\\
Asana Goals also features AI-powered capabilities. Users can describe what they want to achieve in plain language, and Asana AI will draft a fully formed SMART goal (Specific, Measurable, Attainable, Relevant, Time-bound) based on that description. Additionally, the Smart Status feature uses AI to generate status update drafts, summarizing progress and blockers for stakeholders in moments.\\
\\
Goal visibility and sharing are highly configurable. Goals can be made public to the entire organization or restricted to specific team members. Enterprise users benefit from granular sharing permissions, and comment-only access can be granted to prevent unintended edits. External guests can be given access to individual goals, though they cannot view the full org-level goal structure.\\
\\
To standardize goal-setting across an organization, admins can create reusable goal templates with pre-populated attributes, timeframes, privacy settings, and default members. Time period settings support quarterly, half-year, annual, or custom durations. Custom reporting dashboards — built using pre-built or custom charts such as "Goals by team" and "Goals by status" — provide a real-time overview of goal health across the organization.\\
\\
Asana Goals is available on Asana Advanced, Enterprise, and Enterprise+ plans, as well as legacy Business and Enterprise tiers.](https://www.g2.com/products/asana/subproducts/asana-goals/reviews) [![Asana Intelligence (AI)](https://images.g2crowd.com/uploads/product/image/2a3c6c39bf8b8eb277cfcd42d9bbca1e/asana.png)\\
\\
Asana Intelligence (AI)Asana Intelligence (AI) is a suite of artificial intelligence capabilities built natively into the Asana work management platform, designed to help individuals, teams, and organizations automate routine work, surface insights, and accelerate decision-making. Powered by Asana's proprietary Work Graph® data model — a rich, connected map of an organization's tasks, projects, workflows, portfolios, and goals — Asana AI provides the context needed for AI to take meaningful, structured action across the entire enterprise.\\
\\
Asana AI encompasses two layers of intelligence: features powered by Asana's own proprietary machine learning models (such as recommended projects, priority inbox, and task recommendations), and features powered by generative AI and large language models (LLMs) from leading AI partners including OpenAI, Anthropic, AWS Bedrock, Google Gemini, and Fireworks.ai. Asana automatically selects the right model for each task, so users never need to manage model configuration themselves.\\
\\
At the core of Asana Intelligence are a set of "Smart" features that enhance everyday work management. Smart Status automatically drafts project status updates by pulling from real-time work data to identify risks, open questions, and roadblocks. Smart Summaries generate concise, scannable summaries of tasks, projects, and portfolios — surfacing key action items and highlights without requiring additional meetings. Smart Editor provides AI-powered writing assistance to help users craft clearer, more compelling task descriptions and responses with the right tone. Smart Fields auto-generates custom fields to organize projects and support cross-functional collaboration. Smart Rule Creator allows users to build workflow automation rules using simple, natural language prompts — no technical expertise required. Smart Projects enables rapid creation of customized projects by suggesting descriptions, sections, and custom fields based on the project's name and purpose. Smart Goals analyzes existing goals and offers AI-driven suggestions for improvement, including success criteria, impact summaries, parent goals, and accountable teams. Smart Chat provides a conversational AI interface for asking questions about projects, identifying blockers, and taking action — all without leaving Asana.\\
\\
Beyond Smart features, Asana AI includes three flagship agentic capabilities. AI Teammates are ready-to-go AI agents — with more than 30 prebuilt options for marketing, operations, IT, and industry-specific functions — that work alongside human teams to triage requests, assign work, draft content, and execute workflows. Asana Dash serves as an AI Chief of Staff, routing work to the right AI Teammate and surfacing the user's next best action from meetings, Slack, and email. AI Studio is a no-code workflow builder that combines LLMs with Asana's rules engine, enabling teams to build governed AI automations that intake requests, classify and route tasks, and send alerts — all tailored to their unique processes.\\
\\
Asana AI also supports AI Connectors and the Model Context Protocol (MCP), an open standard that lets external AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Microsoft 365 Copilot connect securely to Asana's Work Graph, enabling users to search, create, update, and organize work through natural language in the tools they already use.\\
\\
Asana AI is available on paid Asana plans (Starter, Advanced, Enterprise, and Enterprise+). It is built with enterprise-grade security and governance: AI partners do not use customer data to train their models, data access is restricted through precise permissions, and teams remain in the loop at every step to guide AI actions. Admins can enable or disable AI features at the domain level from the Asana admin console.](https://www.g2.com/products/asana/subproducts/asana-intelligence-ai/reviews) [![Asana Portfolios](https://images.g2crowd.com/uploads/product/image/2a3c6c39bf8b8eb277cfcd42d9bbca1e/asana.png)\\
\\
Asana PortfoliosAsana Portfolios is a project portfolio management (PPM) feature within the Asana work management platform, designed to serve as a mission control center for organizations managing multiple projects and strategic initiatives simultaneously. It provides leaders, project managers, and teams with a centralized, high-level view of all their most important work — from strategy through to execution — in a single place.\\
\\
At its core, Asana Portfolios allows users to group related projects into a single portfolio, making it easy to monitor progress, track health, and communicate status across teams without having to navigate between individual projects. Users can add existing projects to a portfolio with a single click, create new projects directly from within the portfolio view, and even nest portfolios within other portfolios to reflect complex organizational hierarchies.\\
\\
One of the key capabilities of Asana Portfolios is real-time progress monitoring. The portfolio list view displays each project's owner, priority, status, due date, and task or milestone progress at a glance. Color-coded health indicators show which projects are on track, at risk, or off track, enabling managers to quickly identify where attention is needed and drill down for more detail without leaving the portfolio view.\\
\\
Asana Portfolios also features a Portfolio Timeline view, which provides a bird's-eye view of all project start and end dates, milestones, and schedule overlaps across every initiative in the portfolio. Users can drag and drop milestones and projects directly on the timeline to adjust due dates, and the timeline supports up to 1,500 items for large-scale portfolio management.\\
\\
For resource management, the Portfolio Workload view gives a visual snapshot of team capacity and task assignments across all projects in the portfolio. Managers can drag and drop tasks to reassign and reschedule work, move resources between projects in a single view, and visualize future bandwidth to prevent bottlenecks before they occur.\\
\\
Custom field rollups allow users to automatically aggregate numeric data — such as budget, estimated hours, or time spent — from individual projects up to the portfolio level, providing an instant high-level financial and operational overview without manual data collection. Portfolios support up to 100 custom fields and allow multi-filter and multi-sort (up to 10 filters and 10 sorts simultaneously) for granular reporting.\\
\\
Portfolio Dashboards enable users to create custom charts and visualizations — including bar charts, donut charts, lollipop charts, and burnup charts — based on real-time portfolio data. Preset charts include incomplete tasks by project, all projects by status, and upcoming tasks by assignee. Dashboards are prefiltered to include data from the selected portfolio and any nested portfolios.\\
\\
Status updates in Asana Portfolios allow portfolio owners to quickly communicate progress to all stakeholders. The templatized update builder suggests highlights, accomplishments, blockers, and next steps, and allows users to drag and drop visual insights and charts directly into the update. Portfolio members receive status updates directly in their Asana inbox.\\
\\
Privacy and access controls let users set portfolios as public or private, assign portfolio-level roles (admin or editor), and share project access with portfolio members automatically. Portfolios are accessible on both web and mobile (Android and iOS) and are available on Asana's Advanced, Enterprise, and Enterprise+ plans.](https://www.g2.com/products/asana/subproducts/asana-portfolios/reviews)
