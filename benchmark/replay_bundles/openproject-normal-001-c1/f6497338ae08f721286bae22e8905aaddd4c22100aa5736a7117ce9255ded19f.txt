## Project Portfolio Management

### Stay on top of all your projects

- Have an overview of your entire project portfolio - whether for classic, agile or hybrid project management.
- Working on several projects, OpenProject allows you to create a project structure with hierarchies and marking projects as favorites.
- Keep an eye on the status of your projects with the project list or your individual project dashboard.

[Learn about project portfolio management software](https://www.openproject.org/collaboration-software-features/project-portfolio-management/)

![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/project-portfolio-01-gradients-915040ae.svg)![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/project-portfolio-02-icon-6df47fe3.svg)![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/project-portfolio-03-track-0ec94a62.svg)![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/project-portfolio-04-on-34941cbe.svg)![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/project-portfolio-05-lines-21595461.svg)![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/project-portfolio-06-backscreen-d6346673.svg)![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/project-portfolio-07-frontscreen-f9eb2cf2.svg)

## Project Planning & Scheduling

### Deliver your projects on time

- Activate the experience of your entire team to collaboratively create and visualize your project plan.
- Define your project objectives and break it down into smaller deliverables.
- Analyze required activities and create a detailed plan that shows how and when the project will provide the deliverables defined in the project scope.
- Plan your team member's priorities and workload in the team planner.

[Learn about project planning and scheduling software](https://www.openproject.org/collaboration-software-features/project-planning-scheduling/)

![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/project-planing-01-gradients-2bee682b.svg)![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/project-planing-02-circle-80066e8d.svg)![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/project-planing-03-calendar-60bc2d7c.svg)![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/project-planing-04-lines-d046a748.svg)![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/project-planing-05-backscreen-7d3f0450.svg)![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/project-planing-06-frontscreen-98712302.svg)

### Gantt charts

Create a project management plan that visualizes how and when the work packages and milestones will be delivered. Generate a timeline collaboratively with the team.

[Learn more](https://www.openproject.org/docs/user-guide/gantt-chart/)

### Work packages

Work packages are project items with specific attributes and customizable identifiers (numeric or project-based). Assign them to project teams to easily plan, organize, and track work.

[Learn more](https://www.openproject.org/docs/user-guide/work-packages/#work-packages)

### Relations and hierarchies

You can assign relations on functional or timely levels (e.g. successor or predecessor, blocked by, part of, etc.) as well as hierarchies (parent-child relationship).

[Learn more](https://www.openproject.org/docs/user-guide/work-packages/work-package-relations-hierarchies/#work-package-relations)

### Scheduling

Choose between automatic or manual scheduling mode to schedule your work packages.

[Learn more](https://www.openproject.org/docs/user-guide/gantt-chart/scheduling/)

### Work week

Define working days and non-working days to schedule start and finish date of work packages during working hours.

[Learn more](https://www.openproject.org/docs/user-guide/work-packages/set-change-dates/#working-days)

### Resource management﻿

Plan and allocate resources across projects based on availability and capacity. Balance workloads and identify overallocations before they impact delivery.

[Learn more](https://www.openproject.org/docs/user-guide/resource-management/)

### Schedule and availability

Set your working hours, holidays, and availability to improve scheduling accuracy and support realistic resource planning across projects.

[Learn more](https://www.openproject.org/docs/user-guide/account-settings/schedule-and-availability/)

### Baseline comparison﻿

Display changes to work packages tables and get a quick overview of what has changed over time, making it easy to report on project progress and status.

[Learn more](https://www.openproject.org/docs/user-guide/work-packages/baseline-comparison/)

### Calendar

The calendar displays work packages in calendar form. Keep an eye on all deadlines and subscribe via iCalendar format.

[Learn more](https://www.openproject.org/docs/user-guide/calendar/#calendar)

### Date alerts﻿

Date alerts allow you to receive a notification when a start or finish date of a work package is approaching.

[Learn more](https://www.openproject.org/docs/user-guide/notifications/notification-settings/#date-alerts)

### Team planner﻿

Get an overview of which team member is working on what and when tasks are due and schedule work accordingly.

[Learn more](https://www.openproject.org/docs/user-guide/team-planner)

### Organization

Organize users into organizations to simplify administration, improve collaboration, and reflect your company's structure across projects.

[Learn more](https://www.openproject.org/docs/system-admin-guide/users-permissions/organization/)

## Agile, Kanban & Scrum

### Achieve more with agile project management

- Agile teams have short development cycles. They iterate fast to build, measure and learn with each version.
- With fast user feedback they simply waste no time and can concentrate on building the right things. OpenProject is the perfect tool for agile teams that want to use agile methodologies such as Scrum.
- Agile teams can create and estimate stories, prioritize a sprint backlog and track tasks. It is tightly integrated into other modules such as roadmap planning, bug tracking and task management.

[Learn about agile project management software](https://www.openproject.org/collaboration-software-features/agile-project-management/)

![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/agile-kanban-scrum-01-gradiens-6352aa4d.svg)![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/agile-kanban-scrum-02-icon1-36141e22.svg)![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/agile-kanban-scrum-03-icon2-d0bfe6cf.svg)![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/agile-kanban-scrum-04-lines-e4f3a96a.svg)![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/agile-kanban-scrum-05-backscreen-07e27e2d.svg)![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/agile-kanban-scrum-06-frontscreen-eea44f66.svg)

### Kanban Boards

A Kanban board for status updates. With a status board you can visualize and continuously improve the workflow.

[Learn more](https://www.openproject.org/docs/user-guide/agile-boards/#action-boards)

### Version Boards

The Version Board facilitates the planning of a product development in several iterations. With each iteration you can add and improve features.

[Learn more](https://www.openproject.org/docs/user-guide/agile-boards/#version-board)

### Boards for subprojects

The agile board for subprojects enables the distribution and visualization of workload across different (sub)projects and organizations.

### Work breakdown structure

The board for parent-child elements allows you to create and visualize a work-breakdown structure for all of your projects.

### Backlogs

Specify, record and prioritize user stories in a product or sprint backlog with your scrum team to start work by priority.

[Learn more](https://www.openproject.org/docs/user-guide/backlogs-scrum/)

### Sprint boards

To support daily scrum meetings, all of a team’s tasks and sprint impediments are recorded in a digital sprint board. The sprint board provides a clear overview of the current state of a sprint.

### Sprint sharing﻿

Coordinate multiple teams with shared sprints across projects. Ideal for scaled agile approaches such as SAFe, enabling synchronized planning and delivery.

Enable teams to work in parallel, each managing their own sprint while maintaining a shared product backlog.

## Security

### Best protection for your data

- As a company based in the European Union we are highly committed to data security and privacy and take all required action to protect your data and keep your digital sovereignty.
- We meet all data protection requirements in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and even beyond.
- We are a firm believer in open-source and invested heavily in the freedom of users. This encompasses the software freedoms granted by the GPLv3.

[Learn about secure project management software](https://www.openproject.org/security-and-privacy/)

![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/secure-data-dunkel-01-gradiens-88db6dfe.svg)![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/secure-data-dunkel-02-shield-d2699c40.svg)![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/secure-data-dunkel-03-lock-350983b4.svg)![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/secure-data-dunkel-04-lines-3a09b7fc.svg)![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/secure-data-dunkel-05-backscreen-8c1a7c01.svg)![](https://www.openproject.org/assets/images/product-tour/art/secure-data-dunkel-06-frontscreen-320d6420.svg)

### Secure authentication

Secure authentication mechanisms and password rules according to current industry standards. Passwords are securely stored using salted bcrypt.

[Learn more](https://www.openproject.org/docs/security-and-privacy/statement-on-security/#authentication-and-password-security)

### 2-Factor authentication

Use your mobile phone, an app-based authenticator, or WebAuthn as second factor for authentication to prevent anyone from accessing your account.

[Learn more](https://www.openproject.org/docs/system-admin-guide/authentication/two-factor-authentication/)

### Single sign-on (SSO)﻿

With the single sign-on feature you can securely access OpenProject. Control and secure access to your projects with the main authentication providers.

[Learn more](https://www.openproject.org/docs/system-admin-guide/authentication/authentication-faq/#which-authentication-providers-are-supported-for-single-sign-on)

### LDAP sync﻿

Synchronize OpenProject users and groups with your company’s LDAP to update users and group memberships based on LDAP group members.

[Learn more](https://www.openproject.org/docs/system-admin-guide/authentication/ldap-connections/)

### Data encryption

Our cloud environment is continuously backing data, encrypted in transit (via TLS/https) and at rest (files, database (including backups) via AES-256).

[Learn more](https://www.openproject.org/legal/data-processing-agreement/technical-and-organizational-data-security-measures/)

### Antivirus scanning﻿

Uploaded files will be scanned for viruses with ClamAV antivirus engine and quarantined or deleted if viruses are found (only for on-premises installations).

[Learn more](https://www.openproject.org/docs/system-admin-guide/files/attachments/virus-scanning/)

### Security badge

See the status of your installation and learn whether new releases or security updates are available to administrators.

[Learn more](https://www.openproject.org/docs/system-admin-guide/information/#security-badge)

### Capture external links﻿

Display a warning message to inform users when they are leaving OpenProject via an external link.

[Learn more](https://www.openproject.org/docs/system-admin-guide/system-settings/external-links/)
