## Frequently asked questions

### **What is ClickUp and how is it different from other tools?**

ClickUp is The Everything App for work, combining projects, docs, chat, dashboards, and AI—including Brain and Super Agents—in one platform. That keeps work and its context together in one place.

### **How can ClickUp help my team?**

ClickUp gives your team one place to plan, track, and collaborate. Flexible views help people work their way, while automations and AI handle busywork.

### **What tools does ClickUp integrate with?**

ClickUp integrates with 1,000+ tools, including Slack, GitHub, Google Drive, Zoom, HubSpot, and more. It also supports an API, webhooks, and ClickUp MCP.

### **What are ClickUp's most popular features?**

Popular ClickUp features include Tasks, Docs, Chat, Dashboards, Automations, Brain, and Super Agents. Teams can use them together in one workspace.

### **Are ClickUp's advanced features too complicated for non-technical teams?**

ClickUp offers multiple view options and interface modes so every team member works in the way that suits them best—from simple List and Board views for basic task management to advanced Gantt Charts and Dashboards when you need them. You control the complexity level for each user with Custom Roles and Permissions, ensuring everyone sees only the features relevant to their work.

### **Can ClickUp replace multiple tools to reduce our tech stack costs?**

ClickUp replaces your separate project management, docs, goals, chat, and time tracking tools with one unified platform, eliminating the need for multiple subscriptions. This consolidation reduces costs while keeping all your work connected and accessible in one place, so your team spends less time switching between apps and more time getting work done.

### **Do I have to build everything from scratch when starting with ClickUp?**

ClickUp includes 1,000+ pre-built templates that let you start working in minutes, not hours. Browse templates by use case—from project management to marketing campaigns—select one that fits your needs, and customize it as your team grows. You can also use ClickUp AI to automatically generate tasks, docs, and workflows based on your goals.

### **How easy is it to migrate from my current tool to ClickUp?**

ClickUp includes built-in importers for tools like Asana, Trello, Jira, Monday.com, Notion, and spreadsheets. Free training and webinars, along with 24/7 support, help teams make the switch.

### **How does AI work across ClickUp's features?**

ClickUp Brain and Super Agents work inside tasks, docs, chat, and dashboards with full workspace context. They can answer questions, write, and run work automatically.
