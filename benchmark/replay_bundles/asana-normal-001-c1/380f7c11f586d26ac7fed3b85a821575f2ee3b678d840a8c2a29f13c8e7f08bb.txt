### Frequently asked questions about task management tools

##### What is an AI request?

An AI request is one successful unit of work executed by AI Teammates or Asana Dash, which share a single pool of AI requests. Administrative tasks in Dash are included at no extra cost. You're only charged when work gets done; failed, timed-out, or canceled tasks don't count.

For Dash, AI requests only count when Dash completes complex work (e.g., integrations, web search, long-form content generation) or hands off the work to an AI Teammate.

##### What is the contract term for my paid AI requests?

Paid usage packs are valid for your entire billing cycle. For monthly customers: packs expire at month's end. For annual customers: packs can be used anytime across your contract term with no monthly resets. On-demand, pay-as-you-go billing is also available, billed monthly for exactly what you used with no upfront commitment.

##### How do I purchase additional AI requests?

You have two options. Prepaid usage packs at $0.50 per request, valid for your full billing cycle. On-demand, pay-as-you-go billing at $0.60 per request, billed monthly in arrears.

##### How do I monitor and control my costs?

Your admin console shows a live dashboard of requests consumed, remaining balance, and usage by user and AI Teammate. Alerts fire at 65%, 85%, and 100% of your allotment. Admins can set a spending cap on on-demand billing and toggle AI request access per user at any time.

##### Can I change or cancel my plan?

You can cancel at any time, and if you cancel before your next renewal, you'll retain access to paid features through the end of your current subscription period. To cancel your paid plan, log in to your Asana account and access your admin console.

##### How many users are included in my plan?

Asana offers flexible subscription options based on the features you need and the size of your team. For smaller teams, we have plans designed for 2 members.

##### How is AI Studio priced?

AI Studio is available on Starter, Advanced, Enterprise, and Enterprise+ plans.

AI Studio is available in three options:

- **AI Studio Basic:** Included access to AI Studio to explore how AI can help take on routine, manual work (includes rate limits).

- **AI Studio Plus:** Paid access to AI Studio to level up productivity for individuals and small teams (available on monthly & annual plans).

- **AI Studio Pro:** Paid access to AI Studio to scale complex workflows and business operations with advanced billing controls (available on an annual plan).


To get started, ensure your admin has Asana AI enabled. From there, all members of your domain will be able to build smart workflows. [Learn more here](https://help.asana.com/s/article/ai-studio).

##### How can I add people to my plan?

You can grow your team by adding seats in increments as follows:

- **Between 2 and 5 total users:** add seats in increments of 1

- **Up to 30 total users:** add seats in increments of 5

- **Up to 100 total users:** add seats in increments of 10

- **Up to 500 total users:** add seats in increments of 25

- **More than 500 users:** add seats in increments of 50
